AGAPAY OVERVIEW

Official name: AGAPAY: A Workforce Development Career Management System for PHINMA AU Graduates.

AGAPAY is a web-based career and employment platform created for PHINMA Araullo University graduates and alumni, partner employers, and the Team Employer Engagement office. It replaces separate manual spreadsheets, individual emails, and scattered messages with one organized system for career profiles, verified accounts, job opportunities, applications, interviews, communication, notifications, and employment monitoring.

PRIMARY USERS

Jobseekers are PHINMA AU graduates or alumni who can build a career profile, upload credentials and a resume, browse and save jobs, apply for available positions, track applications, receive interview schedules, communicate with employers after an employer starts a conversation, view verified companies.

Employers can register a company account, submit business credentials for verification, maintain a company profile, save job postings as drafts, publish jobs after verification and profile completion, manage job posts, review applicants, update application statuses and hiring stages, schedule interviews, and communicate with jobseekers.

Administrators or TEE staff review jobseeker and employer credentials, approve, reject, or place verification requests on hold, manage users, review system records, view dashboard analytics, and manage archived system content.

CURRENT SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS

AGAPAY is designed for users in the Philippines and is focused on PHINMA AU Main, AU South, and AU San Jose graduates and alumni. Only registered and verified companies are presented as verified employers. AGAPAY does not process payroll or salary distribution and is not directly integrated with external job platforms such as LinkedIn or Indeed.

The current backend code is the source of truth for working features. Older project documents may describe features as unfinished even when those features are already implemented in the current system.


AGAPAY NAVIGATION AND FEATURES

Public pages include the landing page, Job Offers, individual job details, Companies, and company details. Signed-in jobseekers use a Jobseeker Dashboard, Job Search, My Profile, My Applications, Bookmarks, Companies, Messages, Notifications, and Settings. Employers use an Employer Dashboard, Post Job, Manage Jobs, Applicants, For Interview, Hired, Declined, Archived Jobs, Company Profile, Messages, Notifications, and Settings. Authorized administrators use a dashboard, verification review pages, User Management, job and application management, edit requests, archives, reports, notifications, and system logs. Exact menu positions can vary by screen size and login state.

AGAPAY DOES NOT REPLACE HUMAN DECISIONS

Employers decide who advances, is interviewed, or is hired. AGAPAY records and communicates authorized decisions. Administrators or TEE staff decide credential verification outcomes. The chatbot can describe the process but cannot decide, guarantee, or perform these actions.

JOB MATCHING AND RECENT JOB OFFERS

A personalized job recommendation list may prioritize job posts matching a jobseeker's profile, skills, education, course, experience, and preferred work information. Newly published jobs do not automatically appear in every personalized recommendation list. To browse more jobs, use Job Search or Job Offers and review available filters. Do not tell a user that a specific job was excluded without checking authorized system records.

EMPLOYMENT MONITORING AND REPORTING

AGAPAY tracks career and application activity, and supports employment-status-related requests and records through applicable system workflows. Administrators may review system summaries and generate authorized records and reports. The portal is not a payroll service and does not guarantee that all employment outcomes outside AGAPAY are captured.
