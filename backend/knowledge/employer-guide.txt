EMPLOYER REGISTRATION

Employer registration collects the representative's name, company name, website, business email, mobile number, location, industry, company logo, and required business credentials.

The current employer registration requires all of these documents:

1. SEC Registration.
2. BIR Registration.
3. DTI Registration.
4. City or Municipality Permit.
5. Business Permit.

After registration, the employer account remains under review until the administrator completes verification.

EMPLOYER APPROVAL AND LOGIN

When an employer is approved, AGAPAY can activate the account, generate credentials, send them to the registered email address, and require a temporary password change. Rejected verification can include reasons and a message. A hold status may identify a document that must be corrected and resubmitted through a time-limited link.

COMPANY PROFILE

The employer can maintain company information such as company name, industry, website, business email, phone number, address or location, description, logo, cover photo, gallery images, social links, company size, benefits, and other supported details.

A complete company profile is required before a non-draft job can be published. An unverified employer may save a job as a draft but cannot publish the job until the company has been verified by an administrator.

EMPLOYER DASHBOARD

The employer dashboard provides access to job postings, applicants, application status groups, interviews, messages, notifications, company information, and account settings. Exact dashboard cards depend on the current frontend page.

MANAGING APPLICANTS

1. Open Applicants or the applicants page for a specific job.
2. Select an applicant.
3. Review the submitted profile, copied resume information, application details, application activity, and employment indicators.
4. Update the application status when appropriate.
5. For applicants in For Interview, update the hiring stage or interview schedule.
6. Send a professional message when follow-up is needed.
7. Use Hired, Declined, and archived declined pages to manage completed outcomes.

An employer can update only applications that belong to that employer's job postings. The backend prevents an employer from changing an applicant to a non-declined status when the applicant is already hired through another job application.


EMPLOYER JOB POSTING WORKFLOW

1. Sign in to the approved employer account.
2. Complete the required Company Profile fields and verification steps.
3. Open Post Job.
4. Enter job title, vacancies, salary details, location, work setup, educational requirements, description, qualifications, required skills, benefits, and deadline as requested by the form.
5. Review the preview and choose Save Draft or Publish when allowed.
6. Use Manage Jobs to view or manage the posting.

FORM VALIDATION GUIDANCE

Current AGAPAY posting requirements have included limits such as job title up to 100 characters; vacancy count up to 50; salary amounts up to six digits; job description and qualifications between 500 and 2,000 characters; required skills and perks up to 50 characters each; complete address up to 150 characters. The visible form and server validation control the final accepted values. Do not instruct employers to bypass form rules.

MANAGE JOBS AND JOB EDIT REQUESTS

Employers can manage their own drafts, published, closed, filled, and archived jobs using actions available for each record. Some changes to existing job information may require an administrator-reviewed edit request. Only the approved or specifically requested section should be editable during the associated edit flow. An edit request may be Pending, Approved, or Declined depending on the implemented review. Do not promise instant approval or publication. Check Manage Jobs and Notifications for the outcome.

APPLICANT SCREENING AND RESUME SNAPSHOT

Open Applicants or Job Applicants for the correct posting. Application Details can display submitted profile and resume information captured at application time. Later changes to the jobseeker's current profile should not silently replace the previously submitted application record.

INTERVIEW, HIRE, AND DECLINE

An employer can advance eligible applicants to For Interview, assign default or custom hiring stages, schedule or reschedule interviews, select Hired, or decline candidates with the required reason. Hired, Declined, For Interview, and Archived Declined views represent different workflows; actions available depend on the record's status. When vacancies are filled, new applications may be restricted.

EMPLOYER COMMUNICATION AND NOTIFICATIONS

Employers can initiate an eligible conversation about an application, attach supported files within upload limits, and receive notifications for new applicants, edit requests, jobs, and other events. A notification can link to the relevant job or applicant. The chatbot cannot send a message, read a private thread, change hiring decisions, or schedule an interview on the employer's behalf.

EMPLOYER SETTINGS AND ACCOUNT SECURITY

Use account settings for the fields currently offered. Keep company credentials, passwords, and verification documents private. Do not send these to Agap-AI. If a feature is disabled pending account approval, follow the verification and support process rather than trying to bypass it.

EXACT EDIT REQUEST RULE (VERIFIED FROM BACKEND)
For a published job, regular editing is available during its first hour after publication. Once the first hour has passed, a locked published post requires an employer edit request unless the job has an active temporary approved editing window. In Manage Jobs, request permission and select one or more sections: Job Details, Requirements & Qualifications, Skills & Benefits, Work Locations, Salary, Deadline. Explain why editing is needed (maximum 500 characters), then submit for administrator review. Only one pending request can exist per job. Approved editing must be confined to the authorized requested sections; an approved edit is not automatic publication or permission to change unrelated fields. Do not guarantee approval.

WHO CAN MESSAGE FIRST
The employer can initiate a conversation only when an application exists involving that employer and jobseeker. A jobseeker can reply after the employer's first message, but cannot start a new thread.
