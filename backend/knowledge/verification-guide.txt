VERIFICATION PURPOSE

AGAPAY uses administrator review to confirm that jobseekers are eligible PHINMA AU graduates or alumni and that employers have submitted legitimate business credentials. Verification is not fully automatic; TEE or administrator staff review the submitted documents.

VERIFICATION STATUSES

Jobseeker and employer verification records can use states such as Not Submitted or Unverified, Pending, Hold or On Hold, Verified, and Rejected. Individual documents may also have Not Submitted, Submitted, Pending, Approved, Rejected, or Hold states.

JOBSEEKER REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

1. CV or resume.
2. Diploma.
3. Valid ID.

Optional jobseeker documents are Transcript of Records, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and TIN.

EMPLOYER REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

1. SEC Registration.
2. BIR Registration.
3. DTI Registration.
4. City or Municipality Permit.
5. Business Permit.

PENDING

Pending means the credentials were submitted and are waiting for administrator review.

VERIFIED

Verified means the administrator approved the credentials. When a new account is approved, the system can activate the account, generate a username and temporary password, send credentials by email, and require the user to change the temporary password.

REJECTED

Rejected means the administrator did not approve the verification request. The system can store rejection reasons and a message and send the user an email. The user should follow the provided instructions before attempting another submission.

HOLD AND DOCUMENT RESUBMISSION

Hold means a selected credential needs correction or clarification. The administrator selects the document and enters a reason. AGAPAY creates a secure token and sends a resubmission link by email. The current hold flow creates a link that expires after 48 hours. After the corrected document is submitted, the verification can return to the review process.

ADMINISTRATOR SECURITY

Administrator verification routes are protected by administrator authentication. Accessing sensitive credential files can require the administrator password before the document is delivered.


EMAIL REGISTRATION AND VERIFICATION

Registration may use email verification with a one-time code before the account is created or submitted for review. The user should use the official email/OTP form, follow the resend cooldown shown by the interface, and never share the OTP with Agap-AI. Email ownership verification is separate from administrator approval of submitted school or business documents.

JOBSEEKER VERIFICATION GUIDANCE

Jobseekers submit the required CV/resume, diploma, and valid ID using the authorized registration or resubmission flow. Other credentials may be optional. Administrators review the documents and can approve, reject, or request correction. Until the account is eligible and approved, job applications can be restricted.

EMPLOYER VERIFICATION GUIDANCE

Employer registration requires the prescribed SEC, BIR, DTI, City/Municipality Permit, and Business Permit documents per the current AGAPAY flow. The administrator reviews them. Employer draft posting and actual publication have different verification requirements.

PER-DOCUMENT REVIEW AND ON HOLD

An administrator may mark specific submitted documents approved or needing resubmission, and provide reasons or messages. Approved documents should not be described as needing re-upload without a new authorized request. On Hold indicates corrective action is pending; it does not mean the account has been approved. After users correct the requested documents through the official link, staff review them again. Do not guarantee the same 48-hour link will remain valid beyond its actual expiry.

WHEN REGISTRATION IS APPROVED

Depending on the established registration workflow, approved accounts can receive activation/login instructions at their registered email address. Users must follow the specific instructions actually sent. Never disclose or infer passwords, temporary credentials, or OTP codes through chatbot messages.

WHAT IF VERIFICATION IS DELAYED OR REJECTED?

Check the email used during registration and the verification status available in the application. Read the official reasons or correction instructions. Use the official resubmission link where provided or contact TEE/AGAPAY support. The chatbot cannot check the individual case or override an administrative decision.

ADMIN SCOPE

Authorized administrators use separate Jobseeker Verification and Employer Verification pages and document detail views. Sensitive files and review actions require appropriate authorization. Agap-AI can explain these steps but must never access verification documents, perform approval, or bypass authentication.
