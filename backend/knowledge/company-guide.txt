VERIFIED COMPANIES

Public company listings and company detail pages use verified employer records. A jobseeker can search and view verified companies and review company information such as company name, industry, location, address, logo, website, description, social links, benefits, gallery, job openings, and review summary when those fields are available.

SAVED COMPANIES

Authenticated jobseekers can save a verified company, check whether a company is saved, view saved companies, remove one saved company, or remove all saved companies.

COMPANY REVIEWS

A jobseeker can submit a review for a verified company through the protected review route. Review information may include process rating, response timing, total process days, outcome, whether the jobseeker would apply again, role applied for, and a written message when supported by the current frontend.

Company reviews describe a user's recruitment experience. They do not represent an official endorsement by AGAPAY or PHINMA AU.


BROWSING AND REVIEWING A COMPANY

1. Open Companies or Jobseeker Companies.
2. Search or filter using the available company information.
3. Open a company to view its description, industry, location, contact links, gallery, benefits, open jobs, and reviews where available.
4. Use All Jobs or All Reviews when shown for additional entries.

SAVING A COMPANY

A signed-in jobseeker can use the save action on an eligible company and view or remove saved companies using the available saved-company controls. Saving a company is not the same as applying to one of its jobs.

REVIEWS AND RATINGS

Reviews relate to recruiting experience and may cover responsiveness, process duration, an application outcome, and written feedback. Rating summaries are based on submitted reviews, not a guarantee of company quality. A company with no reviews must not be described as having a positive or negative rating. The current form's allowed inputs and character limits should be followed. Employers may be able to view reviews through employer review pages but cannot use Agap-AI to change or manufacture reviews.

EMPLOYER COMPANY PROFILE

Employers can maintain their own company information and upload or update permitted logo, cover, and gallery images. A complete profile is required for publishing, subject to verified credentials. Some values, including contact fields, may be read-only in the current interface; the chatbot should tell employers to use available editable controls and support channels instead of claiming every field is editable.

COMPANY AND JOB LISTINGS

Verified company visibility and open-job visibility are separate conditions. An employer can have a verified company profile without an active published vacancy. An expired, filled, closed, archived, or draft job should not be presented as accepting applications.
