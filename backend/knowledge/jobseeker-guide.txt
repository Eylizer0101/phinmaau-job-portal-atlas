JOBSEEKER REGISTRATION

A jobseeker registration collects career information such as course, campus, graduation year, preferred work mode, technical skills, soft skills, previous activities or experience, and availability to start. It also collects the jobseeker's name, email address, and phone number.

The required registration documents are:

1. CV or resume.
2. Diploma.
3. Valid ID.

The optional registration documents are Transcript of Records, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and TIN documents. Submitted credentials initially enter a pending verification state for administrator review.

JOBSEEKER APPROVAL AND LOGIN

After the administrator verifies the account, AGAPAY activates the account and sends the username and temporary password to the registered email address. The jobseeker must change the temporary password after logging in.

If verification is rejected, the system can send the rejection reasons and message by email. If a specific document needs correction, the administrator may place the request on hold and send a time-limited resubmission link.

JOBSEEKER PROFILE

The jobseeker profile may contain personal information, contact information, education, campus, course, graduation year, preferred work mode, salary expectation, technical and soft skills, work experience, projects, certifications, seminars, awards, affiliations, co-curricular activities, profile image, and resume information.

The jobseeker can update profile data, add or edit work experience, upload a resume, preview or download a generated resume, and manage available account settings. Sensitive actions such as resume download may require password verification.

HOW TO APPLY FOR A JOB

1. Sign in using a verified and active jobseeker account.
2. Complete the jobseeker profile and make sure the Profile Credentials section contains a CV or resume.
3. Open Job Offers or Job Search.
4. Search or filter jobs by available information such as keyword, location, field, salary, employment type, experience, education, or work mode.
5. Open the job details and review the description, requirements, deadline, vacancies, skills, location, salary information, and employer details.
6. Select Apply Now.
7. Review the application information presented in AGAPAY before submission.
8. Submit the application.
9. Open My Applications to track progress.

An application cannot be submitted when the jobseeker account is not verified, the profile has no CV in Profile Credentials, the job is not active or published, the application deadline has passed, the vacancy is already full, or the same jobseeker has already applied to the same job.

SAVED AND RECOMMENDED JOBS

A jobseeker can save an available published job and later view it in Bookmarks or Saved Jobs. The backend also calculates recommended jobs from information in the jobseeker profile, including course, skills, experience, projects, certifications, and other profile content. Recommendations indicate matching opportunities but do not guarantee qualification or hiring.

COMPANIES

Jobseekers can browse verified companies, open company details, save or remove companies from saved companies, and submit a company review through the verified-company page when the system permits the review.

CAREER GUIDANCE

Agap-AI may provide general guidance for resume preparation, interview preparation, profile improvement, professional communication, and steps that can improve a jobseeker's chances of being hired. It must not claim that these tips guarantee employment.


PROFILE AND RESUME COMPLETION

AGAPAY may require Basic Information, Personal Information, Career Objective, Work Experience, Skills, and Education for a complete resume. Optional fields can include certain physical information or a second degree, depending on the form. The career objective must contain meaningful text. Follow any highlighted required-field messages and save changes before applying. The resume preview displays information from the profile; personal details should be checked for correctness before submission.

RESUME SNAPSHOT BEHAVIOR

An employer reviewing an earlier application can see the resume/profile copy preserved at application time. Editing My Profile later is not a guarantee that the original application snapshot changes. This protects accurate application history.

JOBSEEKER DASHBOARD AND RECOMMENDATIONS

The dashboard may show recommended or recent job offers based on profile-matching signals. A newer employer posting does not automatically appear if it does not match the current jobseeker's information or is not eligible for listing. For jobs beyond personalized recommendations, open Job Search or Job Offers and use filters.

JOB SEARCH, JOB DETAILS, AND APPLY

Use filters to narrow results; review all job information including requirements, employer details, location, deadline, vacancies, and salary. Check that the job is open and the account and credentials are eligible. If application submission fails, confirm whether it was saved before attempting again. Never share personal documents in Agap-AI chat.

MY APPLICATIONS

My Applications is the primary place to review submitted jobs, statuses, activity, and permitted withdrawal actions. An expired or closed job may still have historical details visible there even though new applications are blocked. Open an application record to see its available information.

COMPANIES, BOOKMARKS, AND REVIEWS

A jobseeker can explore verified companies, open their available jobs, save jobs or companies, and view or write supported reviews. Reviews are user experiences and do not guarantee employer behavior or recruitment outcome.

MESSAGES AND NOTIFICATIONS

Messaging about an application is initiated by the employer; after that, the jobseeker can reply. Notifications may alert the jobseeker about jobs, messages, statuses, and interviews. The chatbot has no private-message or notification-reading capability.

PROFILE IMAGE AND ACCOUNT SETTINGS

Users may be able to upload, crop, change, or remove a profile image using the interface, and manage eligible account settings. For password changes use the official settings or reset-password flow. Never enter passwords or OTPs into the chatbot.

CAREER GUIDANCE

Resume guidance within My Profile, profile improvement, skill descriptions, interview preparation, and professional communication related to AGAPAY applications are within Agap-AI's scope. No advice guarantees job matching, interview selection, or hiring.

WITHDRAWING AN APPLICATION (CURRENT BACKEND)
An eligible application can be withdrawn from My Applications. Withdrawal is permanent: the current backend rejects reactivation/restoration. The earlier resume snapshot remains attached to the application history. Do not tell the user to tap Reactivate or apply again to the same job as a workaround.

AGAPAY-ONLY APPLY ANSWER
When asked "how to apply", "how to apply and what should I do first", or similar, begin with an active verified Jobseeker account and a completed profile/CV in Profile Credentials. Direct the user to Job Offers or Job Search, Job Details, Apply Now, and My Applications. Never replace these steps with searching external job advertisements, setting up LinkedIn, writing a cover letter, or updating profiles outside AGAPAY.
