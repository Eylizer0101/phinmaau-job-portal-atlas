JOB POSTING STATUS

The current Job model recognizes Draft, Published, Filled, and Closed.

DRAFT

A draft is not published and is not active. An employer may save a draft even before company verification is complete. Draft fields may be incomplete until the employer is ready to publish.

PUBLISHED

A published job is active and can appear in job listings when it is not archived and its other availability conditions are satisfied. Publishing requires an employer account, a complete company profile, and verified employer credentials.

CLOSED

A closed job is inactive and does not accept new applications. The employer can reactivate an eligible closed job, which changes it back to Published.

FILLED

A filled job has reached its available vacancy count or was otherwise marked filled. It does not accept new applications. Hiring another applicant beyond the vacancy limit is prevented.

POSTING A JOB

1. Ensure your Employer account is approved.
2. Complete the company profile.
3. Open Post Jobs.
4. Enter the required job information, including title, description, requirements, job type, work mode, vacancies, deadline, location, province, city or municipality, education, experience, skills, salary settings, benefits, and other supported fields.
5. Save as Draft or Publish.

Publishing creates administrator and matching-job notifications when the related backend conditions are met.

MANAGING A JOB

An employer can view the employer's own jobs, edit a job, activate or close it, archive it, restore it, and permanently delete it through authorized routes. Archiving makes a published job inactive. Restoring a published archived job makes it active and Published; restoring an archived draft keeps it inactive and Draft.

SAVED JOBS

Only jobseekers can save jobs. The job must exist, be published, active, and not archived. Saving the same job again returns that it is already saved. Jobseekers can remove saved jobs and view all saved jobs.

RECOMMENDED JOBS

Recommended jobs are calculated from profile keywords such as course, study field, education, skills, experience, projects, certifications, seminars, awards, affiliations, and preferred work information. A recommendation is an aid for discovery, not a hiring decision.


JOB LISTING FILTERS AND DETAILS

Job Search and Job Offers can show available published positions with filters such as keyword or title, location, employer, work arrangement, educational requirements, salary, experience, and other available criteria. Exact options depend on the screen. Job details may show title, employer, industry, job location, vacancies, deadline, salary details, qualifications, requirements, benefits, and work setup.

WORK SETUP VS EDUCATIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Work setup refers to where/how the work is performed, such as Remote, On-site, or Hybrid/Blended when offered. Educational Requirements describe study/qualification expectations. These are different fields, and users should not confuse them with job publication status.

JOB APPLICATION DEADLINE AND VISIBILITY

An expired application deadline can prevent new submissions, even if a previously submitted application remains visible in My Applications. Public listings and recommended lists may have different filters; check the specific job detail for current eligibility. Job postings can appear in employer history even after closure or archiving.

EMPLOYER EDIT REQUESTS

Some updates to job details require a submitted edit request and administrator review. The requested fields or section determine what can be edited. The request can be reviewed and Approved or Declined; the user should look in Manage Jobs and Notifications for the actual outcome. Agap-AI cannot approve or edit a job itself.

BOOKMARKS AND JOB MATCHES

Bookmarks are jobseeker-saved positions, not applications. Recommended jobs can be prioritized using user profile signals; not all newly posted jobs are guaranteed to appear in an individual's recommendation area. Browse Job Search or Job Offers for a wider selection.

POSTING VALIDATIONS

Job posting forms enforce required inputs and length or value limits. Employers should follow displayed field validation and correct any highlighted issues rather than bypassing them. Avoid inventing unsupported salaries, work mode options, or job availability.

PUBLISHED JOB EDIT WINDOW
The employer may directly edit a published job within the first hour of publication. After that, the backend locks editing unless temporary permission is active. A locked post needs an administrator-reviewed edit request specifying exactly which sections need changing, along with a reason of no more than 500 characters. One pending request per job is allowed. The current system has explicit Job Details, Requirements & Qualifications, Skills & Benefits, Work Locations, Salary, and Deadline edit sections. Approved access does not authorize editing sections outside the approved request.

HOW CAN I EDIT MY JOB POST?

From Manage Jobs, select the job post and its Edit action. Published posts can be edited directly within the first hour when allowed. Once locked, submit an Edit Request with only the sections you want to change and a reason (maximum 500 characters). Wait for administrator review and check Manage Jobs and Notifications. Approval unlocks only the authorized sections for the allowed period; do not expect instant approval.
