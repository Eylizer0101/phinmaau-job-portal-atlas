WHAT IS AGAPAY?

AGAPAY is a workforce development and career management system for PHINMA Araullo University graduates and alumni. It connects verified jobseekers with registered employers and helps TEE staff manage verification, job opportunities, applications, communication, interviews, notifications, and employment monitoring in one platform.

CAN AN UNVERIFIED JOBSEEKER APPLY?

No. The current backend requires the jobseeker account to be verified before an application can be submitted. The jobseeker must also have a CV or resume in Profile Credentials.

CAN AN UNVERIFIED EMPLOYER POST A JOB?

An unverified employer can save a job as a draft. Publishing requires verified employer credentials and a complete company profile.

WHAT DOES PENDING MEAN?

Pending is the default application status after submission. It means the application is waiting for employer review.

WHAT DOES FOR INTERVIEW MEAN?

For Interview means the employer moved the application into an interview or screening process. The employer can assign hiring stages and schedule or reschedule an interview.

WHAT ARE THE DEFAULT HIRING STAGES?

The default stages are Initial Interview, Assessment, Final Interview, and Job Offer.

CAN A JOBSEEKER MESSAGE AN EMPLOYER FIRST?

No. The current messaging rule requires the employer to start a new conversation. After the first employer message, the jobseeker can reply.

CAN A JOBSEEKER WITHDRAW AN APPLICATION?

Yes. A jobseeker can withdraw an active application. The system stores the last active status before making the application Withdrawn.

CAN AN INACTIVE APPLICATION BE REACTIVATED?

No. The current application controller rejects reactivation with WITHDRAWAL_PERMANENT. Withdrawal is permanent and the application cannot be restored. The jobseeker can still review the application history in My Applications.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A VACANCY IS FULL?

The job can be marked Filled and stops accepting new applications. Other affected applications may be updated or notified using the Vacancy Full flow.

HOW DOES INTERVIEW SCHEDULING WORK?

The employer opens the applicant record, uses the available interview scheduling option, selects a future date and time and the interview format supported by the current system, reviews the details, and saves the schedule. The jobseeker should check Messages and Notifications for confirmed interview details. Do not promise an automatic video link or calendar invitation.

DOES AGAPAY PROCESS SALARIES?

No. AGAPAY can display salary information in job posts, but it does not distribute payroll or salary payments.


WHY DOES A NEW JOB NOT APPEAR IN MY RECOMMENDED JOBS?

Recent or recommended jobs on a personalized jobseeker dashboard can prioritize matches based on course, skills, resume or profile information, experience, and preferences. A new employer posting might not match that particular profile, or the post might not yet meet listing conditions. Use Job Search or Job Offers to browse available jobs more broadly. Do not claim that a specific employer's job is absent for one exact reason without checking the source data.

DOES A JOB MATCH SCORE MEAN I WILL BE HIRED?

No. Matching only supports discovery. The employer reviews the actual application and decides on screening, interviews, and hiring.

WHY CAN'T I APPLY?

Check that you are signed in as an active, approved jobseeker; required profile credentials and resume are complete; the job is published and accepting applications; its deadline has not passed; vacancies are available; and you have not already applied. If the page shows an error, retry once and contact support if it continues.

I CHANGED MY PROFILE. DID MY OLD APPLICATION RESUME CHANGE?

An application can preserve resume/profile details from submission time. New profile edits do not necessarily alter previously submitted application snapshots. Inspect the application view for the saved version.

CAN I APPLY AGAIN AFTER WITHDRAWING?

No. The backend rejects attempts to reactivate withdrawn applications. Do not promise that a Reactivate option will work.

WHY CAN'T I MESSAGE AN EMPLOYER FIRST?

AGAPAY requires the employer to initiate the conversation about an eligible application. Once the employer has started, the jobseeker can reply.

WHERE CAN I SEE MY INTERVIEW DETAILS?

Check the interview information in AGAPAY Messages and Notifications. Review the date, time, interview format, and employer instructions. If details are missing, ask the employer through your existing AGAPAY conversation. Do not assume that an automatic meeting link is generated.

WHAT IF AN INTERVIEW DETAIL IS MISSING?

Review the latest interview message or notification, then contact the employer through AGAPAY Messages to clarify the time, location, or joining instructions. Do not invent a link or schedule.

WHY CAN'T I PUBLISH A JOB?

An employer generally needs verified business documents, a complete company profile, and required valid job details. Saving a draft may be available first.

WHAT DOES REQUEST RESUBMISSION MEAN?

An administrator may request correction of specific verification documents. Follow the official emailed resubmission link and its deadline; do not send private documents through chatbot messages.

WHERE DO I SEE MY APPLICATION STATUS?

Jobseekers: My Applications. Employers: Applicants, For Interview, Hired, or Declined for their own jobs. Agap-AI cannot look up a person's live record.

CAN THE CHATBOT SEND AN APPLICATION OR CHANGE MY HIRING STATUS?

No. Use the appropriate authorized AGAPAY page. The chatbot provides guidance only.

CAN AGAP-AI WRITE PROGRAMMING CODE OR SHOW THE DATABASE?

No. Agap-AI is restricted to AGAPAY usage help and approved job/career guidance. It must not write or debug source code or expose private internal implementation.

CAN AGAP-AI HELP WITH MY RESUME OR INTERVIEW?

Yes. It can offer clear job-related tips, interview preparation guidance, and explanations of AGAPAY resume or application workflows, without guaranteeing hiring.

WHY IS MY ACCOUNT STILL PENDING?

Submitted registration and verification documents must be reviewed by authorized staff. Check the registered email and the official status page. Agap-AI cannot approve or inspect the pending request directly.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN JOB STATUS AND APPLICATION STATUS?

Job status describes availability of the vacancy (such as Draft, Published, Closed, or Filled). Application status describes the candidate's process (such as Pending, For Interview, Hired, or Declined).

WHAT SHOULD I DO IF A PAGE FAILS TO LOAD OR A REQUEST FAILS?

Check internet connectivity, refresh once, verify you are signed in, retry the action only if it is safe to do so, and check whether the action succeeded before submitting it again. Contact AGAPAY/TEE support if the issue continues. Do not share passwords, OTPs, or private document contents in the chatbot.

IS THE COMMUNITY PAGE STILL AVAILABLE?

No. The Community page was removed from AGAPAY. You can still use the existing Messages and Notifications features where available. Agap-AI must not instruct users to open Community or create posts and comments.

HOW TO APPLY AND WHAT SHOULD I DO FIRST?
From your approved AGAPAY Jobseeker dashboard, Complete the required My Profile resume sections and make sure your CV is available under Profile Credentials. Then open Job Offers or Job Search, review a job in Job Details, select Apply Now, check the submission information, and submit. Track the result under My Applications. AGAPAY does not require using LinkedIn, Indeed, or a cover letter as part of these steps.

HOW CAN I IMPROVE MY JOB SEEKER RANKING?

To improve your job seeker ranking, follow these steps:

1. Complete Your Resume Profile – Make sure all required sections of your resume are complete.
2. Provide Accurate Information – Enter accurate and relevant information to the best of your knowledge.
3. Include Your Educational Background – Provide your education details and qualifications.
4. Highlight Your Skills and Work Experience – Include your relevant skills, work experience, and certifications.
5. Keep Your Profile Updated – Regularly update your resume to improve your visibility to employers and increase your chances of being hired.

HOW CAN I TRACK MY JOB APPLICATIONS?

You can track your job applications by following these steps:

1. Open the Applications section in your AGAPAY account.
2. Check the status of each application, whether it is Pending, For Interview, Hired, or Declined.
3. Check whether the employer or recruiter has viewed your application.
4. Regularly monitor this section to stay updated on your application progress and any changes in status.

HOW CAN I RATE A COMPANY ON AGAPAY?

You can rate a company by completing the Application Rating process. Follow these steps:

Step 1: Agree to the Application Rating Notice
Read the notice carefully and agree to proceed with the rating.

Step 2: Select the Applied Role
Choose the position you applied for at the company.

Step 3: Complete the Application Rating Form
Provide the required information:

1. Days to First Response: Enter the number of days before the company first responded to your application.
2. Total Process Length (Days): Enter the total number of days the application process took.
3. Application Process Rating: Rate your experience with the company's application process.
4. Would You Apply Again?: Select Yes or No.
5. Outcome: Select Received Offer, Ghosted, Withdrew, or Still in Process.
6. Your Review: Share your honest feedback about your application experience.

Step 4: Confirm Your Rating
Review your answers and confirm that all information is accurate before submitting.

Step 5: Successful Submission
Once submitted successfully, a confirmation message will appear to inform you that your application rating has been recorded.

HOW CAN I EDIT MY JOB POST?

To edit your job post, follow these steps:

1. Click the Edit Icon – Open your job post and click the Edit icon.
2. Submit an Edit Request – Send an edit request to the administrator.
3. Select the Section to Modify – Choose the section you want to edit.
4. Provide a Clear Reason – Explain why you need to make the requested changes.
5. Review and Submit – Review your edit request carefully before submitting it.
6. Wait for Admin Approval – The administrator will review your request and determine whether the changes can be approved.
