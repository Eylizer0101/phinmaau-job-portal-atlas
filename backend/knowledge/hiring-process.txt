AGAPAY HIRING FLOW

A typical hiring flow is:

1. A verified jobseeker submits an application to an active and published job.
2. The application begins as Pending.
3. The employer reviews the application and submitted profile or resume.
4. The employer may move the applicant to For Interview.
5. The employer may schedule or reschedule an interview and manage hiring stages.
6. The employer may eventually change the application to Hired or Declined.
7. When the vacancy count is reached, the job may become Filled and remaining affected applications may be handled as Vacancy Full.

APPLICATION STATUS AND HIRING STAGE ARE DIFFERENT

Application status describes the overall result or position of the application, such as Pending, For Interview, Hired, or Declined.

Hiring stage describes the employer's internal step while an application is in For Interview. Hiring stages can be updated only for applicants whose application status is For Interview.

DEFAULT HIRING STAGES

The current default stages are:

1. Initial Interview.
2. Assessment.
3. Final Interview.
4. Job Offer.

CUSTOM HIRING STAGES

An employer can add a custom hiring stage. The backend prevents duplicate stage names. A custom stage must be added before it can be selected for an applicant.

An employer can also delete a custom stage. Default stages can be hidden and later restored. If a stage is deleted, applications currently using that stage are reset to no stage.

UPDATING OR RESETTING A STAGE

1. Open the For Interview applicant list or the relevant application.
2. Select Change Hiring Stage.
3. Select an existing default or custom stage.
4. Save the update.

The employer may reset the hiring stage to no stage. Stage changes are added to the application activity history. When the stage is Job Offer, the activity title is recorded as Offered.


EMPLOYER SCREENING PAGES

Applicants and Job Applicants present eligible applications for review. For Interview focuses on interview/screening cases. Hired and Declined display corresponding decisions, while archived declined records may have their own archive controls. Use the applicant's latest displayed status and activity to determine which workflow is applicable; do not promise that every button exists on every page.

SCREENING AND DECLINE REASONS

Employers should review the applicant's submitted information and select appropriate reasons when declining. A decline may include an optional comment. A hiring-stage change is not itself a hire or rejection. Employers should use professional, job-related criteria and avoid discriminatory decisions.

RESUME VERSION AT APPLICATION TIME

Application Details and authorized history views can show the resume or application information saved when the candidate applied. This helps keep past submissions auditable even if the candidate later updates their profile.

FILLED VACANCIES AND OTHER APPLICATIONS

When hires reach the job's vacancy count, the position can be marked Filled and remaining affected active applications may be treated as Vacancy Full under backend rules. Do not imply every other applicant is immediately Hired, Declined, or Withdrawn.

JOB OFFER VS HIRED

A Job Offer hiring stage does not mean the applicant is officially Hired. The employer must complete the appropriate status change for Hired to be recorded.

EMPLOYMENT STATUS FOLLOW-UP

The platform may use employment-status requests and history for graduate employment monitoring. These records are distinct from an employer's normal job application status. A chatbot should explain where to review a request but never approve, fabricate, or update an employment status.
